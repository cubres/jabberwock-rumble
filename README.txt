JABBERWOCK RUMBLE
Akane Owari vs. Nekomaru Nidai — Island Fight Club

PLAY OFFLINE
Keep this folder together, then double-click index.html to open the game in
a modern browser. No installation, account, network connection or build step
is needed. Pick your looks and difficulty, then start the fight.

YOU ARE AKANE
Nekomaru is controlled by the computer. Win two rounds to win the match.
Each round lasts 60 seconds. A knockout wins immediately; at the time limit,
the fighter with more health wins. Equal health is a draw and awards no win.

KEYBOARD
A / D or Left / Right arrows: move
W, Up arrow or Space: jump
J: attack — tap repeatedly to chain two punches and a finishing kick
K: hold to block; release it to move or attack
L: ultimate attack when your meter is full
Esc / P: pause or resume

TOUCH
Use the arrow, jump, GUARD, HIT and ULT buttons below the arena.
Hold movement or GUARD; tap jump, HIT and ULT. Multiple touches let you move
and jump or attack together. Landscape orientation gives the arena more room.

KEEP IT SIMPLE
Close the distance before attacking. Punches only connect within reach.
Hitting, taking hits and guarding fill your ultimate meter. A block just before
impact gives a perfect guard, a big meter boost and a chance to counterattack.
Nekomaru's yellow heavy-attack warning means jump, block or step out of range.
His armored heavy attacks keep going through ordinary punches. Your ultimate
and a perfect guard can interrupt them. Save the ultimate for a clean opening.

PICK YOUR LOOK
Akane: Classic, Island athlete, Sunset sprinter.
Nekomaru: Classic, Mechamaru.
All skins are cosmetic; they have the same moves and stats.
Difficulty choices are Easy, Normal and Hard.

MONOKUMA'S SURPRISES
Moon Jump: temporary low gravity makes jumps much higher and floatier.
Coconut Chaos: red landing zones warn you before coconuts drop. Move or jump
clear; blocking reduces their damage.
Turbo Time: both fighters move and attack faster.
Snack Attack: collect the rice balls to restore health and gain meter.
Both fighters are affected. Watch Monokuma's message and the event timer.

OPTIONS
The sound button toggles synthesized arcade sound effects.
REDUCE FLASH & SHAKE in HOW TO PLAY reduces visual effects.
HOW TO PLAY opens the in-game control guide. Pause offers resume and a return
to fighter selection; round and match screens let you continue or play again.
